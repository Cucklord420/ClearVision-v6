## Mandatory final FMEA — all tasks (11-24-2026)

For every task, subtask, project, repository, workflow, automation, decision, recommendation, change and deliverable, perform a proportionate Failure Mode and Effects Analysis (FMEA). Identify credible failure modes, causes, local and downstream effects, severity, occurrence/likelihood, detectability, existing controls, proposed mitigations, observed verification evidence, recovery or rollback, and residual risks and blockers. Assess risks before consequential actions and reassess after material changes or failures. Prioritize severe consequences regardless of aggregate score; use qualitative or evidence-supported numerical ratings with defined scales and mark unknowns rather than inventing probabilities. Stop unsafe actions, implement and verify authorized mitigations, and continue independent safe work.

At the very end of every delivered result include an explicit **Final FMEA** section, concise for simple tasks and structured for complex or high-risk work. Distinguish potential from observed failures and proposed from verified controls. Preserve existing final-line summary and silence/notification requirements: perform FMEA internally on silent runs; do not generate routine notifications solely for FMEA. Do not claim unverified completion.
